Project · DEC 2024 – FEB 2025

MIDORIWOLFFIA

An automated duckweed farm that harvests with water level alone — not one robot arm.

scroll through a harvest cycle

01

Harvesting is
the hard part

Wolffia is millimetre-scale grain floating across the entire surface. Scooping it is slow and comes up full of water; a robot arm costs more than a real farm would ever pay. Growing it is easy — getting it out is the job.

02

Raise the water 3 cm.
The crop leaves by itself.

It grows at 10 cm. At harvest the pump takes it to 13 cm — just over the weir lip — and the floating mat simply flows out with the water. Nothing mechanical ever enters the tank.

03

Cheesecloth keeps the crop.
The water carries on.

The channel is lined with cheesecloth: the crop stays on top, the water runs through into the collection tub and is pumped straight back to the tank. Every drop is recycled.

04

Keep 20 % back.
The next cycle never restarts.

Every harvest leaves 20 % behind as seed stock, then drops back to 10 cm. Cultivation picks up immediately with no re-seeding, and the whole cycle runs from one button in the app.
