MEASUREREBUILD

Measure the real part, rebuild it parametrically, then make the assembly actually move.

scroll through the steps

01

It starts with
a real part and a caliper.

There is no drawing to work from, only the part. Every dimension gets measured, and you decide which of them actually define the design and which are just what the process left behind.

02

Change one dimension,
the whole part follows.

It goes up as sketches with real constraints, then extrude, revolve and sweep on top. Change a single dimension afterwards and everything referencing it moves with it.

03

A model that moves
is a model that is right.

A jaw that opens takes two mates working together — the lead screw turns on a revolute, the jaw runs on a slider. Get the geometry wrong and it binds or passes through itself immediately.

04

Several parts,
each a different problem.

The set covers a machine vise, a toggle clamp on a jig, a push-pull clamp, a flanged fixture, a mobile step ladder and a chair frame — bent tube across several planes, lead screws and multi-link mechanisms.
