One brain wasn't enough

Asking one processor to solve coordinates and pulse every motor at once wrecks the timing. So I split the job.

02

The Pi thinks.
The ESP32s act.

A Raspberry Pi solves the inverse kinematics and sends commands over I²C to one ESP32 per joint, each generating its own step and direction pulses.

  • 0x08AXIS 1
  • 0x09AXIS 2
  • 0x0AAXIS 3
  • 0x0BAXIS 4
