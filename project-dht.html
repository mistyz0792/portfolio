ONE READINGTHREE SINKS

One sensor publishing to three platforms at once, to find out what each is actually for.

scroll to follow one reading

01

Reading a value and
keeping it are different jobs.

A DHT22 wired to an ESP on a breadboard, with a local OLED so the value exists without a network at all — and disappears the moment you look away, unless something stores it.

02

One value,
three destinations at once.

The same value goes to ThingsBoard, ThingSpeak and Google Sheets together, so you can see what each does with it — a live dashboard, a chart you can take into MATLAB, and a raw table that never fills up.

03

Past the threshold,
the bot messages you.

A humidity threshold at 70 % fires an automatic Telegram message, so a chart nobody is watching can still get someone's attention.

04

Over a thousand
readings, logged straight through.

More than a thousand readings were logged continuously, which is what makes the comparison worth anything — the answer comes from running all three, not from reading their marketing.
