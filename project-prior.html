PRIORSOLUTION

R&D IoT for agriculture: cut the manual labour, without losing the economics that make it worth building.

scroll through a dosing cycle

01

The solution weakens daily.
Someone has to go and measure it.

The crop pulls nutrient out of the water continuously, so EC drifts down and pH creeps up. The manual answer is a person walking tank to tank with a meter and topping up by hand — which does not scale to a house this size. That is exactly the labour the brief asked to remove.

02

Hit the threshold
and A and B inject themselves.

Once EC falls below setpoint, two dosing pumps inject concentrate A and B into the mixing pot in ratio. The EC and pH probes sitting in that pot read back continuously, and dosing stops when the values are inside band again. Nobody walks to the tank.

03

Corrected once.
Delivered to every bench.

The corrected solution leaves the mixing pot along the main and branches into every bench, so the entire house lands on one consistent value in a single pass — something hand-mixing can never make uniform.

04

One cabinet
runs the whole house.

Breakers, a power supply, a PLC, a bank of relays and an ESP32 all share the back plate. The door carries an HMI, twenty-four buttons and an emergency stop. I designed both the panel layout and the 3D model of the house it controls.
