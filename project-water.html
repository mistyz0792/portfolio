FOURZONES

Four zones watered independently — by hand, on a schedule, or by letting the soil decide.

scroll to watch the automatic mode

01

Beds do not
dry out together.

Sun, wind, what is planted and the soil itself all differ bed to bed. Watering everything at once leaves some waterlogged and others still dry, so each zone has to be measured and driven on its own.

02

Below the threshold,
that zone's relay closes.

Soil-moisture sensors read continuously. When a zone drops below its configured minimum, that one relay closes, that one nozzle runs, and it stops again at the maximum. The other three are never touched.

03

The next zone
does exactly the same.

Four relays are four unrelated circuits. Every zone carries its own daily schedule and its own moisture band, and adding a zone is just another relay and another sensor.

04

Controlled from a phone,
from anywhere.

The Blynk dashboard shows live moisture for all four zones alongside their relay state, and switches between manual, scheduled and automatic from a single screen.
